About Me

Creative Designer

Hi, I'm Merna Adel, a creative designer and artist with a passion for visual storytelling and handmade crafts.

I work with Adobe Illustrator to create digital designs and illustrations, and I enjoy both digital and traditional drawing. In addition to graphic design, I create handmade floral arrangements using satin ribbons and pipe cleaners, combining creativity with craftsmanship to bring unique ideas to life.

I am always eager to learn, explore new techniques, and transform ideas into meaningful and visually appealing creations.
